[ Oarfish dragon ]

Scientific Name:

Oarfish Dragon also known as Serpentirex pelagicus

Discovered by / at:

Sofiya Smirnova, 3:00am October 1st 2026

Family:

Part of the Pelagicus family, the Oarfish dragon has dated back thousands of years to underwater dragons who live in the deep sea and only come up for fresh food such as tiny fish.

Habitat:

Deep Sea, The Oarfish Dragon can be found lurking in dark cold waters to protect itself from sunlight.

Appearance:

The Oarfish dragon has a long body and multiple fins, as well as two anntenne, it changes color when touched, the color also depends on its mood.

Key Characteristics:

  • swims only in dark waters.
  • Eats small fish.
  • Changes its color depending on its mood
  • Comes up to the surface only for feeding and can go without food for a month

What we know so far about [ The Oarfish Dragon ]? The Oarfish dragon also known as Serpentirex pelagicus can be found in very deep waters. The creature mostly eats small fish and and micro bacteria such as algee found on the sea floor or in more shallow waters. The creatures makes an ascension every month just to feed, so this is one of the only times humans can see it unless you go deep sea diving. The creature is friendly to humans and it can be touched, if touched the creatures changes color.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Human Interaction with The Oarfish dragon is okay as long as you do not touch its antenne, touching the body and the tail of the creature is okay and they will even change color depending on how the creature feels. Be aware of the underwater pressure and the side affects of being in the deep sea, the Oarfish dragon doesnt consume humans so its safe to interact with it but can get aggressive if annoyed.
